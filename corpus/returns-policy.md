# Returns policy

Northwind Storage lets you return an unused plan within 30 days of purchase. Returns made after 30 days are not eligible for a refund, but you can cancel auto-renewal at any time.

Refunds go back to the original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within 5 business days. Bank transfers can take up to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

To start a return, open Billing, select the plan, and choose Request refund. Partial refunds are not available for annual plans. Monthly plans are prorated to the day you cancel.

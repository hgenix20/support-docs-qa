# Account security

Two-factor authentication is available for every plan. Turn it on under Settings, Security. Recovery codes are shown once; store them somewhere safe.

If you lose access to your authenticator, use a recovery code to sign in, then register a new device. Support cannot disable two-factor authentication without verifying your identity, which can take up to 2 business days.

Sessions expire after 14 days of inactivity. You can review and revoke active sessions from Settings, Devices.

# Storage limits

The Starter plan includes 50 GB of storage and up to 3 devices. The Team plan includes 500 GB and up to 25 devices, with admin controls for removing members.

A single file can be at most 5 GB. Files larger than that must be split into parts before upload. Trash items count toward your quota until they are emptied, and emptied items are removed permanently after 30 days.

If you exceed your quota, uploads stop but existing files stay readable. Free up space or upgrade the plan to resume uploads.
